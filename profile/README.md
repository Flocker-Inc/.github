# Flocker

**Real Life, Right Now.**

Social media built a prison and gave it a like button.
Flocker was built to set the world free.

---

## What We're Building

Flocker is the world's first geo-social event marketplace — 
the operating system for real-life connection.

Not social media (the past). Not event apps (the future).
**The present. What's happening nearby, right now, instantly joinable.**

Think Uber for real-life moments: swipe, see what's happening 
near you right now, and go.

---

## The Problem

Since mobile became ubiquitous, loneliness, depression, and suicide 
rates among young people have doubled. The U.S. Surgeon General links 
3 hours/day on social media to the health equivalent of 15 cigarettes. 
One in three Americans reports feeling lonely weekly.

The apps engineered this. Flocker is the antidote.

---

## The Mission

Flocker, Inc. is a Delaware C-corporation building a real-life 
connection marketplace for Gen Z and beyond.

Its sister organization, **Flocker Research, Inc.**, is a 
nonprofit AI safety research lab — chartered from day one to 
advance AI "in ways that are safe, responsible, and beneficial 
to all humanity."

We are not aware of any other consumer app that launched with 
a nonprofit AI lab as a sister organization. That's intentional.
